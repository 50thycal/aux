# Evidence

Drop the `.jsonl` transcripts downloaded from the app's evidence panel here, one per
session, named for what you were testing (`2026-09-07-queue-ordering.jsonl`).

The transcripts themselves are gitignored — they are large, they are specific to one
account's devices, and the conclusions belong in `docs/SPOTIFY_SPIKE_RESULTS.md`. Keep
them locally as the backing evidence for the verdicts in that file.
